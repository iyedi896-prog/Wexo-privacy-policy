Wexo Enhancer

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

This policy explains how the Wexo Enhancer Chrome extension handles information when you use its features for TikTok-related video workflows.

1. Information the extension may process

Depending on the features you choose to use, Wexo Enhancer may process:

  • Telegram account identifier: if Telegram-based access verification is enabled, the Telegram ID you enter may be sent to the service operated for Wexo Enhancer to check access or link your request to your Telegram account.
  • Video and upload information: video files, captions, or upload-related information that you deliberately select or enter while using TikTok. These may be handled by TikTok and by the browser as part of the workflow.
  • Basic technical information: information necessary for the extension to function, diagnose errors, and protect the service, if such logging is enabled on its server.

Do not enter passwords, verification codes, or other sensitive information into Wexo Enhancer unless an official feature explicitly requires it and explains why.

2. How information is used

Information is used only as needed to provide the extension's requested features, verify access when Telegram login is enabled, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical problems. Wexo Enhancer should not use personal information for unrelated advertising or sell it to data brokers.

3. Video files and TikTok

Wexo Enhancer is designed to assist with a TikTok video workflow. When you choose a video or submit content to TikTok, that content may be processed by your browser and TikTok under their own terms and privacy policies. This policy does not replace TikTok's policies. Do not select or upload a video unless you have the right to use it.

The extension's actual behavior depends on the installed version and the features you use. Before publishing this policy, the developer should confirm whether any video, caption, or upload metadata is transmitted to a Wexo-operated server.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be transmitted to TikTok when you use TikTok's website and to the Wexo Enhancer server only where required by an enabled feature, such as access verification. Telegram may process information when you interact with a Telegram bot. Each third-party service handles information under its own policies.

Wexo Enhancer should not share personal information with other parties except to provide a feature you request, meet a legal obligation, or protect the service and its users.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the feature it supports. Server-side retention, access controls, and deletion practices depend on the Wexo service configuration. The developer should document and apply appropriate safeguards and avoid storing Telegram IDs or logs longer than needed.

6. Your choices

  • You can stop using the extension and remove it from Chrome.
  • You can choose not to use optional Telegram-based features.
  • For a request to access or delete information handled by the Wexo service, contact the developer using the contact method provided in the Chrome Web Store listing.

7. Children's privacy

Wexo Enhancer is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information was collected from a child inappropriately, contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the extension's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Wexo Enhancer developer through the support contact listed on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
